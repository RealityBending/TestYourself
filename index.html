Why do this

Know thyself.

We actually know very little about ourselves, and spend little time thinking about it. This test is an opportunity to take a few minutes to engage in introspection, think about your own thoughts, feelings and behaviours, and see how it compares to others.

  • You get told what it means. Every level you finish opens a chart, and a reading of where you sit against other people.
  • It is scientifically measured, not guessed. Validated questionnaires, real scores.
  • Confidential. We don't collect IP, cookies, nor personal information. Your answers are anonymous, and used to deepen our understanding of human nature.

Before you begin

Information and consent

Informed Consent

Invitation to take part

Thank you for considering taking part in this study, conducted by Dr Dominique Makowski from the University of Sussex (see contact information below). Taking part is entirely up to you, and you can decide not to without giving a reason.

Why have I been invited, and what will I do?

The study looks at how people differ in the way they describe their own personality traits, their mood over the past two weeks, and the sensations they notice in their body. You will answer a series of short questions on screen, one at a time, mostly by picking a point on a scale. The whole thing usually takes 10 to 15 minutes to complete. Please make sure that you are attentive and in a quiet environment, and that you have the time to complete it in one go. You must be 18 or older to take part.

What will happen to the results and my personal information?

The results of this research may be written into a scientific publication. Your anonymity will be ensured in the way described in the consent information below. Please read this information carefully and then, if you wish to take part, please acknowledge that you have fully understood this sheet, and that you consent to take part in the study as it is described here.

The feedback you are shown along the way is for interest only. It is generated from brief questionnaires scored against reference values, and it is not a diagnosis, an assessment or advice. If you are worried about your mood, your health, or anything the questions bring up, please speak to your doctor or another qualified professional.

Consent

  • I understand that my participation is entirely voluntary, that I can choose not to participate in part or all of the study, and that I can withdraw at any stage without having to give a reason and without being penalised in any way (e.g., if I am a student, my decision whether or not to take part will not affect my grades).

  • By participating, I agree to follow the instructions and provide honest answers. If I do not wish to participate, or don't have the time, I can simply close my browser.
